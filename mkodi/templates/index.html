Innovation Idea

MKodi (Mobile-Kodi)

A mobile-first digital platform designed to bridge fragmented government data and the informal economy in Tanzania through zero-rated access and simple USSD onboarding.

Tagline: The Future of Integrated Revenue Intelligence in Tanzania

Submitted by: Micaih Meshark Nyimbi

Date: 14 July 2026

Executive Summary

MKodi delivers USSD + Zero-Rated Web Portal for easy access, real-time data integration, instant GePG control numbers, STK Push payments, automated risk scoring, and a 360° dashboard for TRA officers.

Problem: Large informal sector, fragmented data, complex tax processes, and high manual workload for TRA officers.

Solution: Pre-filled assessments, secure payments, and compliance-based credit scoring for incentives and formalization.

Key Features

  • Simplified tax filing for informal traders
  • Pre-filled assessments using integrated data
  • Secure payments through GePG
  • Credit score based on compliance history
  • Real-time 360° view for TRA with BRELA, Land, and income data

Strategic Goals

  • Onboard 1,000,000 informal traders within 24 months
  • Reduce TRA manual audit workload by 40%
  • Achieve 100% zero-rated access across major networks
  • Integrate with at least 3 key government systems including BRELA, Land Registry, and Banks

Implementation Phases

  • Phase 1 (Months 1-3): System development, GePG integration, and USSD logic
  • Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Pilot in Kariakoo and Mwanza markets
  • Phase 3 (Months 7-18): Nationwide rollout and scaling

Budget Snapshot

Estimated budget of TZS 50 million across technical development, system integration, marketing, compliance, and contingency.

Expected Impact

The platform aims to significantly expand the tax base, simplify compliance for small businesses, and improve transparent revenue collection with better data-driven decisions for TRA.
