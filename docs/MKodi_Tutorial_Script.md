# MKodi Tutorial Video Script

**Length:** 60-90 seconds

## Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** MKodi logo and the taxpayer slideshow.

**Narration:**
"Welcome to MKodi, a connected taxpayer portal for Tanzania. MKodi brings taxpayer records, revenue intelligence, and payment activity into one simple experience."

## Scene 2: Admin record cabinet

**On screen:** Open `/admin/` and show a taxpayer record.

**Narration:**
"The workflow starts in the admin record cabinet. An administrator enters the official TIN, NIDA, contact, business, BRELA, land, income, compliance, and credit information."

## Scene 3: First-time registration

**On screen:** Signup page. Enter a TIN and click Load details.

**Narration:**
"A taxpayer then enters the same TIN on the registration page. MKodi loads the official record, so the user does not need to type the details again. The taxpayer only creates a secure password."

## Scene 4: Dashboard

**On screen:** Dashboard summary cards.

**Narration:**
"The dashboard gives the taxpayer an immediate overview of compliance, credit score, account status, reminders, and the most important next actions."

## Scene 5: 360-degree view

**On screen:** 360° View page.

**Narration:**
"The 360-degree Revenue Intelligence View presents identity, business and BRELA data, land information, income, compliance, credit score, risk level, and assessment in one place."

## Scene 6: Payments

**On screen:** Payment form, payment history, and receipt.

**Narration:**
"Users can create payment requests through USSD, GePG, or mobile money. Every request receives a control number and appears in payment history with its status and transaction details. A printable receipt is available for each record."

## Scene 7: Profile and conclusion

**On screen:** Profile page, upload photo, and change password.

**Narration:**
"The profile page shows every linked taxpayer detail, supports a profile photo, and lets the user change their password. MKodi makes fragmented records easier to understand and easier to act on."

## Recording notes

- Open `docs/MKodi_Tutorial_Storyboard.html` in a browser for an auto-advancing presentation.
- Use screen recording software such as Xbox Game Bar with `Win + G` or OBS Studio.
- Keep the browser at 1280x720 or 1920x1080.
- Use the narration above while advancing through the app.
