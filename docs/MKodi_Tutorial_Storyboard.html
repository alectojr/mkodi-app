MKodi / product tutorial

One connected view of the taxpayer.

MKodi is a digital portal for taxpayer records, revenue intelligence, and payment activity.

01 / Admin setup

Start in the admin record cabinet.

1. Open /admin/ and create a taxpayer record.
2. Add TIN, NIDA, phone, business, BRELA, land, income, and scores.
3. Save the record. The TIN becomes the account link.
02 / Registration

Let the taxpayer register safely.

On Signup, the taxpayer enters the TIN and loads the official details. They only choose a password; admin data stays the source of truth.

03 / Dashboard

Give the taxpayer a clear starting point.

The dashboard summarizes compliance, credit score, account status, reminders, and shortcuts to intelligence and payments.

04 / Intelligence

Show the complete picture.

The 360° View combines identity, business/BRELA, land, income, compliance, credit, risk, and assessment details in one readable page.

05 / Payments

Make payment activity traceable.

Create a request through USSD, GePG, or mobile money. MKodi stores the control number, amount, method, status, and transaction ID, then produces a receipt.

06 / Profile and security

Keep the account personal.

Users can view every linked detail, upload a profile photo, change their password, and see whether their account is linked to an active admin record.

07 / Close

MKodi turns fragmented records into useful action.

It gives taxpayers simpler access and gives administrators a reliable operational view. Demo integrations can later be connected to approved TRA, GePG, NIDA, BRELA, and mobile-money services.
